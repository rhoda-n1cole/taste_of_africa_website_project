Jollof Rice (Nigeria)

A smoky, tomato-based one-pot rice served at weddings and celebrations across Nigeria and Ghana. It comes from the Wolof people of Senegambia, and the question of who makes it best is a famous friendly rivalry across West Africa.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 tomatoes, blended
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tsp thyme and curry powder
  • 3 cups stock
Cooking instructions
  1. Blend tomatoes, pepper and onion; fry with tomato paste until reduced.
  2. Add spices and stock, bring to a boil.
  3. Stir in washed rice, cover tightly and cook on low heat for 30 minutes.
  4. Fluff and serve with fried plantain.

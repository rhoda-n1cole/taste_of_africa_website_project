East Africa

Food culture

East African cuisine mixes highland traditions with Indian Ocean spice-trade influences. Flatbreads, stews, maize porridge and cassava are everyday foods, often shared from a common platter.

Staples & spices

Teff, maize, cassava and beans, seasoned with berbere, cardamom and cloves.

Cooking techniques

Fermenting batters for injera, simmering slow stews, and grilling meat over charcoal (nyama choma).

About Us

Our mission is to promote and preserve African food heritage by sharing recipes, stories and traditions with the world.

A brief history

African cuisine grew from indigenous grains, roots and livestock, then absorbed influences from Arab, Indian, Portuguese and European trade. Through the diaspora, dishes like jollof and okra stews travelled to the Americas and shaped global food.

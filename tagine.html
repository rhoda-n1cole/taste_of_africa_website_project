Chicken Tagine (Morocco)

A slow-cooked stew from Morocco, named after its cone-shaped clay pot. The shape traps steam so the meat stays tender, and the dish is perfumed with saffron and preserved lemon.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 1 tsp ras el hanout
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
Cooking instructions
  1. Marinate chicken with spices and oil for 1 hour.
  2. Layer onions in the tagine, add chicken, lemon and olives.
  3. Add a little water, cover and simmer on low for 1 hour.
  4. Serve with couscous or bread.

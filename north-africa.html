North Africa

Food culture

North African cooking blends Berber, Arab and Mediterranean traditions. Couscous, olives, dried fruit and fragrant spice blends define the table, and meals are a social occasion.

Staples & spices

Couscous, olives and preserved lemons, flavored with saffron, cumin and ras el hanout.

Cooking techniques

Slow cooking in clay tagines, steaming couscous in a couscoussier, and baking bread in communal ovens.

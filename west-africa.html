West Africa

Food culture

West African food is bold, spicy and shared. Rice, yams, cassava and plantain are served with rich stews and sauces, and meals are often eaten communally from one bowl.

Staples & spices

Look for scotch bonnet peppers, palm oil, grains of selim, thyme and curry powder.

Cooking techniques

Slow stewing, charcoal grilling (as in suya) and one-pot rice cooking.

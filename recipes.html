Jollof Rice (Nigeria)

Open full recipe page →

A smoky, tomato-based one-pot rice served at weddings and celebrations across Nigeria and Ghana.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 tomatoes, blended
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tsp thyme and curry powder
  • 3 cups stock
Cooking instructions
  1. Blend tomatoes, pepper and onion; fry with tomato paste until reduced.
  2. Add spices and stock, bring to a boil.
  3. Stir in washed rice, cover tightly and cook on low heat for 30 minutes.
  4. Fluff and serve with fried plantain.

Chicken Tagine (Morocco)

Open full recipe page →

A slow-cooked stew from Morocco, named after its cone-shaped clay pot and perfumed with saffron and preserved lemon.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 preserved lemon
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 1 tsp ras el hanout
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
Cooking instructions
  1. Marinate chicken with spices and oil for 1 hour.
  2. Layer onions in the tagine, add chicken, lemon and olives.
  3. Add a little water, cover and simmer on low for 1 hour.
  4. Serve with couscous or bread.

Injera with Doro Wat (Ethiopia)

Open full recipe page →

A spongy, fermented teff flatbread used as both plate and utensil, served with spicy stews.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups teff flour
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 tbsp berbere spice
  • 500 g chicken
  • 2 onions
Cooking instructions
  1. Mix teff flour and water; ferment 2 to 3 days until bubbly.
  2. Pour batter into a hot non-stick pan in a spiral; cover until set.
  3. Simmer onions, berbere and chicken into a thick wat.
  4. Serve the stew on top of the injera.

Culinary Regions

West Africa

Rice, yams, plantain and peppers define the table. Dishes are bold, spicy and communal.

Staples & spices

Scotch bonnet, palm oil, grains of selim.

Cooking techniques

Stewing, grilling (suya) and one-pot rice cooking.

Example dishes

  • Jollof Rice

North Africa

Couscous, olives, dried fruit and fragrant spice blends shape this Mediterranean-influenced cuisine.

Staples & spices

Saffron, cumin and ras el hanout.

Cooking techniques

Slow cooking in clay tagines and steaming couscous.

Example dishes

  • Chicken Tagine

East Africa

Flatbreads, stews, maize porridge and cassava sit alongside Indian Ocean spice-trade influences.

Staples & spices

Berbere, cardamom and cloves.

Cooking techniques

Fermenting (injera), simmering stews and grilling (nyama choma).

Example dishes

  • Injera with Doro Wat
